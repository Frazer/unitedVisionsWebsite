Volunteer Opportunities and Social Media Tasks

Volunteer Opportunities

3 Minutes

  • Follow us on Twitter and make a Retweet X
  • Follow us on Instagram and comment on your favorite post Instagram
  • Join our Facebook community to connect with amazing people Facebook
  • Subscribe to our YouTube Channel for awesome content YouTube
  • Subscribe to our website (to be created if there's interest)
  • Join our mailing list for events and workshops (to be created if there's interest)
  • Tag a friend after following and commenting—someone who might benefit from a reset

5–10 Minutes

  • Watch our meditation video clip (to be created, titled "Reflect to Reset")
  • Tell us which topics you'd like in future workshops (form link to be added)
  • Share your "Reset Moment" on social media using hashtag #UnitedReset and tag us

15–30 Minutes

  • Attend our workshop to experience deep peace
  • Join the Inner Circle—email marlis@unitedvisions.org or frazer@unitedvisions.org to explore roles
  • Submit a workshop reflection (written or video) to be featured on our platforms
  • Earn “Reset Champion” recognition—workshop credit and monthly spotlight

Note: We may offer a free workshop for completing all 3-minute tasks or any 10 total volunteer opportunities.
